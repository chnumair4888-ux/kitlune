Editorial & Accuracy Standards

How we approach explanations, calculations, tool limitations, privacy statements and corrections.

Our goal

Kitlune publishes practical browser tools and guides for everyday file, writing and financial-planning tasks. Our goal is to explain what a tool does, what assumptions it makes and where its result may differ from a real-world service or professional calculation. We prefer specific examples and visible limitations over broad promises.

How calculator content is checked

Financial calculators are educational estimation tools. We document the main formula or calculation approach on the relevant page, test ordinary inputs and boundary cases, and state important assumptions such as fixed rates, payment frequency and excluded fees. Results are rounded for display, so a lender, bank or other provider may produce a different figure because of fees, taxes, insurance, day-count conventions, compounding rules, payment dates or rounding methods.

Kitlune calculator results are not financial advice, investment advice, a loan offer or a guarantee of future returns. When making a real financial decision, users should compare the estimate with the terms supplied by the relevant provider.

How file-tool claims are checked

For file tools, we describe supported formats, practical limits and processing behavior as precisely as possible. When a tool processes selected files locally in the browser, we say so. We do not describe a feature as automatic when it requires manual input. For example, Scan to PDF currently uses manual crop handles rather than claiming automatic edge detection, and it creates an image-based PDF rather than claiming OCR or searchable text.

Privacy wording

Privacy statements distinguish between the user's selected file data and normal web requests needed to load a page. A local-processing tool can keep selected files in the browser while the page still makes ordinary requests for fonts, scripts or libraries from external providers. Our Privacy Policy explains those distinctions in more detail.

Guide quality

Guides are written to add useful context beyond a tool interface. We aim to include a clear workflow, common mistakes, limitations and worked examples where they improve understanding. We avoid presenting estimates as exact facts and avoid padding pages with unrelated material simply to make them longer.

Corrections and updates

Tools and guides can be improved. If we find an incorrect formula, misleading explanation, broken workflow or outdated statement, we aim to correct the affected page rather than leave known errors in place. If you notice a problem, send the page address, what you expected and what appears wrong to contact@kitlune.site. See the Contact page for useful details to include in a report.

Independence and advertising

Advertising, if enabled, does not change the formulas used by Kitlune tools or the conclusions of our guides. Ads should remain distinguishable from navigation, tool controls and download actions. Kitlune does not ask users to click advertisements to support the site.

Last reviewed
