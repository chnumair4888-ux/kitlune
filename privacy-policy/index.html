Privacy Policy

Read how Kitlune is designed to handle website visits, browser storage and browser-based tools.

Overview

Kitlune provides free browser-based tools. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the website and how our tools handle the content you enter or select. Last updated: 10 October 2026.

Hosting, security and analytics

Kitlune is a static website. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, and request time to deliver and protect the site. Kitlune uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website usage and improve pages. Cloudflare describes this analytics product as privacy-focused and designed not to use individual visitor tracking cookies; technical processing may still occur under Cloudflare's own policies.

Files and text stay in your browser

The Image Compressor, JPG to PDF and PDF Merge tools are designed to process the files you select locally in your browser. The Word Counter and calculators process the text or numbers you enter in your browser. The tools do not upload your selected files or entered content to a Kitlune application backend. PDF pages may download a processing library from jsDelivr, and the site loads typefaces from Google Fonts; those providers receive ordinary requests for their resources and may process request information under their own policies. Keep your browser and device secure, and avoid using any online service for sensitive material unless you are comfortable with its behavior.

Browser storage

The site may use local browser storage to remember your theme preference. The current informational cookie notice does not itself set advertising cookies. Browser settings can clear or restrict local storage, but doing so may reset your preferences.

Advertising and Google AdSense

Kitlune may show ads from Google AdSense. If ads are introduced, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads, subject to Google's policies, applicable law, and any consent requirements that apply. You can learn about or manage advertising personalization in Google Ad Settings. Read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Third-party advertising practices and settings are controlled by those providers and may change over time.

External services and links

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. Those sites have their own privacy notices and practices. Kitlune does not control the policies or security of third-party services.

Your choices

You can use browser controls to clear local storage, limit cookies, and manage third-party resource loading. Blocking external resources may affect fonts or PDF tools. Where applicable, you may also use Google's advertising settings linked above.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@kitlune.site.
