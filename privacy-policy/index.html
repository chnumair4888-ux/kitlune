KITLUNE / PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Read how Kitlune is designed to handle website visits, browser storage and browser-based tools.

Overview

Kitlune aims to collect as little information as practical. This policy describes the current static website and how future tools are intended to work.

Hosting and technical information

The site is hosted as static files. When you visit a website, the hosting provider may process routine technical information such as IP address, request time and browser details in server logs for security and operation. Kitlune does not currently provide user accounts or a custom application backend.

Browser storage

The theme toggle may save your light or dark mode preference in your browser’s local storage. The cookie notice is informational and does not itself set advertising cookies. External font files are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to retrieve those resources.

Files and future tools

The planned file tools are designed to process selected files locally in your browser rather than upload them to Kitlune. Do not use a tool for sensitive material unless its page clearly describes its behavior. Third-party hosting, fonts or future advertising services may have their own privacy practices.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled in the future, the site may use advertising cookies or similar technologies subject to applicable consent and disclosure requirements. This policy will be updated before such services are introduced.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@kitlune.site.
