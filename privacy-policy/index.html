Privacy Policy

How Kitlune handles website visits, browser storage and browser-based tools.

Overview

Kitlune provides free browser-based tools. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the website and how our tools handle the content you enter or select. Last updated: 24 October 2026.

Hosting, security and analytics

Kitlune is a static website. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details and request time to deliver and protect the site. Kitlune uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website usage.

Files and text stay in your browser

The Image Compressor, JPG to PDF, Scan to PDF and PDF Merge tools are designed to process the files you select locally in your browser. The Word Counter and calculators process the text or numbers you enter in your browser. These tools do not upload your selected files or entered content to a Kitlune application backend.

PDF pages, including Scan to PDF, may load the pdf-lib JavaScript library from the public jsDelivr CDN when the tool is used. Your browser therefore makes a normal request to jsDelivr for that library, and jsDelivr may receive ordinary technical request information under its own policies. Kitlune's Scan to PDF code does not send the selected document images to jsDelivr. The site also loads typefaces from Google Fonts.

Browser storage

The site may use local browser storage to remember your theme preference. Browser settings can clear or restrict local storage, but doing so may reset preferences.

Advertising and Google AdSense

Kitlune may show ads from Google AdSense. If ads are introduced, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies subject to Google's policies, applicable law and any consent requirements that apply. No advertising code is added by the Scan to PDF tool itself.

External services and links

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. Those sites have their own privacy notices and practices. Kitlune does not control third-party policies or security.

Your choices

You can use browser controls to clear local storage, limit cookies and manage third-party resource loading. Blocking external resources may affect fonts or PDF tools.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@kitlune.site.
