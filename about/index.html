A little about Kitlune and our approach to useful, simple browser-based tools.

What Kitlune is

Kitlune is a free collection of practical tools for everyday digital tasks, including file conversion, writing checks and financial estimates. It exists to make common jobs easier without unnecessary signups, confusing interfaces or paid tiers.

Our approach

The tools are designed to run in your browser, with clear controls and results you can review before using. No account is required, and the current tools are free to use.

Privacy by design

Privacy is part of the design: file-processing tools work locally in your browser and do not upload your selected files to a Kitlune application backend. The site itself may load resources from providers such as Cloudflare, Google Fonts and jsDelivr; their servers receive requests for those resources. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Simple, free and honest

Kitlune does not promise perfect results or replace professional advice. We keep improving the tools and explain important assumptions where possible. Questions or feedback are welcome at contact@kitlune.site.
