KITLUNE / ABOUT

Small, practical tools with explanations you can check.

Kitlune is an independently maintained collection of free browser tools and original guides for everyday file, writing and planning tasks.

What Kitlune is

Kitlune currently provides eight working tools covering image compression, word counting, JPG-to-PDF conversion, PDF merging, document scanning, loan estimates, compound-interest estimates and savings-goal planning. The site also publishes practical guides that explain the decisions around those tasks instead of treating a calculator or converter as a complete answer.

Why the site exists

Many everyday jobs do not need an account, a large app or a complicated workflow. Kitlune focuses on narrow tasks with clear inputs and outputs. The aim is to help a visitor finish a job and understand the important settings, assumptions or limitations that could affect the result.

How file tools handle your files

Kitlune's current file-processing tools are designed to process selected files in the browser rather than upload them to a Kitlune application backend. The website still loads normal web resources from infrastructure providers, and those providers can receive standard network requests. The Privacy Policy explains this distinction in more detail.

How financial tools should be used

The loan, compound-interest and savings tools provide educational estimates based on the values you enter and the assumptions stated on their pages. They are not lender quotes, investment forecasts or financial advice. Real products can include fees, taxes, rate changes, timing differences and rules that a simple calculator does not model.

How guides are created and reviewed

Kitlune guides are written for a defined user problem and reviewed against the behavior of the related tool. We try to include concrete steps, examples, trade-offs, common mistakes and limitations where they matter. We do not intentionally publish scraped articles or automatically generated pages without review. Read the full Editorial & Accuracy Standards.

Corrections and feedback

If a tool behaves unexpectedly or a guide contains an error, please send the page address, what you expected and what happened to contact@kitlune.site. Useful corrections are reviewed and incorporated into future updates.

Advertising and independence

Kitlune may use advertising to support the cost of maintaining the site. Advertising does not change calculator formulas, tool outputs or the editorial standard applied to guides. Ads, when present, should remain distinguishable from navigation and publisher content.
